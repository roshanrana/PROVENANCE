# A-03 — the caching × determinism decomposition, on SGLang

**Date:** 2026-09-07 · **Repo SHA:** `4b7e45b` · **Card:** NVIDIA H100 80GB HBM3
**Engine:** SGLang, FA3 attention backend (Hopper-only, which is why this needed
an H100) · **Provider:** RunPod Secure Cloud, AP-IN-1

This is the arm ADR-009 exists for. It answers the question the vLLM cost figure
cannot: **is "the cost of determinism" actually the cost of determinism?**

## The 2×2

`Qwen/Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct`, temperature 0, seed 0, concurrency 16, 128 max
tokens, 128 trials per cell. Four independent engine processes, one per cell.

| cell | determinism | radix cache | n | distinct outputs | mean tok/s | median latency |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | off | **on** | 128 | 2 | **326.2** | 0.3868 s |
| B | off | off | 128 | 3 | **366.0** | 0.3556 s |
| C | **on** | **on** | 128 | **1** | **276.5** | 0.4487 s |
| D | **on** | off | 128 | **1** | **300.1** | 0.4287 s |

## The decomposition

| ratio | value | reading |
|---|---|---|
| **D / B** | **0.820×** | **determinism alone costs 18.0%**, cache held off on both sides |
| **C / D** | **0.921×** | the radix cache *costs* 7.9% here rather than paying for itself |
| C / A | 0.848× | both together — 15.2% |

**The headline: on SGLang, determinism costs 18.0% of throughput, and that
number is clean.** It is measured with the cache held off on both sides, so
nothing about prefix caching is folded into it.

### Two things worth reading carefully

**The naive comparison understates the cost, it does not overstate it.** C/A —
the only comparison a vLLM-shaped experiment can make — is 0.848×, while the
isolated determinism cost D/B is 0.820×. The two effects partly cancel, because
the cache is a small *penalty* in this workload rather than a benefit. A
reviewer's instinct that the confounded number must be inflated is wrong here,
and that is exactly why the decomposition was worth running instead of
reasoned about.

**Why the cache costs rather than pays.** Every trial sends the *same* short
prompt, so there is almost no prefix to amortise, while the radix tree still
does its bookkeeping on every request. This is a property of the workload, not a
finding about SGLang: with long shared system prompts — the case that motivates
prefix caching in the first place — the sign would very likely flip. Stated
plainly so nobody quotes "the radix cache costs 8%" out of context.

## The comparison that makes this interesting

| | vLLM | SGLang |
|---|---|---|
| Divergence, determinism off | 34 of 128 distinct | 3 of 128 distinct |
| Divergence, determinism on | **5 of 128** | **1 of 128** |
| Cost of determinism | 22.7% (confounded with cache loss) | **18.0% (isolated)** |
| Determinism with caching | **not possible upstream** | yes — cell C, 1 of 128 |

**SGLang eliminated divergence outright; vLLM did not.** SGLang reaches 1
distinct output of 128 in *both* deterministic cells, including with the radix
cache on. vLLM's batch-invariant mode left 5 of 128 under comparable conditions
(`bench/results/cost-h100-2026-09-07.md`).

That is a real, checkable difference between two implementations of the same
idea, and it is the kind of thing a platform team choosing an engine for a
regulated workload would actually want to know.

## What this does not establish

- **The two engines were not run under identical conditions.** vLLM used its own
  defaults and scheduler; SGLang used FA3 and its own. The divergence *rates*
  (34/128 vs 3/128 with determinism off) differ by more than the determinism
  question alone explains, and this run does not attribute that. **The
  cross-engine cost figures should be read as two separate measurements, not as
  a controlled comparison.**
- **No confidence intervals on the 2×2.** The vLLM cost figure has a bootstrap
  interval; these ratios are point estimates over 128 trials per cell. Running
  them through `attest.analysis.cost` is straightforward and has not been done.
- **One workload.** Short identical prompt, one model, one concurrency, one
  output length. The C/D sign in particular is workload-dependent, as above.
- **Triton not run.** FA3 was used throughout. SGLang's compatibility matrix
  says Triton also supports determinism with the radix cache; whether the cost
  differs is unmeasured.
- **One SGLang version**, installed from PyPI at run time rather than pinned to
  the `lmsysorg/sglang:v0.5.12` that llm-d's manifest targets.
