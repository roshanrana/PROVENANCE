## What this does and does not establish

**Establishes:** on this model, this card and this engine configuration, the
same prompt at temperature 0 with a fixed seed produced **two distinct logprob
vectors** across 32 trials, split 24/8, once batch composition varied. The
default posture is not reproducible. That is the premise the whole of ATTEST
rests on, and it is now measured rather than cited.

**Does not establish:**

- **That batch invariance fixes it.** `VLLM_BATCH_INVARIANT=1` was never set in
  this run. The invariance claim needs its own measurement.
- **What determinism costs.** That is stage 2, and it is exactly what the
  divergence result now justifies spending on.
- **Anything about SGLang**, or about the caching × determinism 2×2 (A-03).
- **A magnitude.** Two digests out of 32 trials on two cells is a *presence*
  result. The rate, its dependence on concurrency, and its confidence interval
  come from the fuller ladder.

## Cost, and what the shakedown bought

Four pods on the A40 at $0.49/hr, roughly 35 GPU-minutes in total — under $0.30.
Three of them failed, which is what the cheap card was for:

1. **v1** — `vllm/vllm-openai` has `ENTRYPOINT vllm`, so RunPod appended the
   start command as arguments and the job became `vllm -lc "..."`. Crash-looped.
   Fixed by moving to `runpod/pytorch` and installing vLLM in the job.
2. **v2** — ran clean to `rc=0`, and proved the delivery design wrong: the whole
   results tree as gzipped base64 was ~180 log lines, and a pod's log stream is
   read back a bounded page at a time. Retrieving it cost more than producing it.
3. **v3** — inlined the job to avoid a repo push, and hit *"Engine core
   initialization failed"* on a different host with a launch line identical to
   v2's. Abandoned rather than debugged: v2's path had actually worked, and
   chasing a host-specific failure on a rented clock is how budgets evaporate.
4. **v4** — v2's path with the base64 filtered out and a compact verdict printed.
   The run above.

Had this sequence happened on the H100 at $3.49/hr it would have cost seven
times as much to learn the same four things.
